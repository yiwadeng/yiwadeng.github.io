Wandering Museum Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

Wandering Museum is an independently developed project, unaffiliated with any institution. It provides a community-built platform for sharing traditional culture through Chinese artifacts dispersed overseas. Hangzhou Yiran Shandi Technology Co., Ltd. operates the project for website registration, accounts, SMS verification and other operational needs. In this policy, “we” refers to Wandering Museum.

We understand the importance of your personal information. We collect only the minimum needed for the service and work to protect it.

1. Information we collect

  1. Account information: the phone number or email you provide when registering or signing in. For third-party sign-in, we receive basic identity information returned by the provider, such as your display name and email. We do not receive your third-party password.
  2. Contribution records: photos and comments you contribute, upload times and associated artifacts. These records form our contribution ledger, used to credit contributions and thank contributors.
  3. Technical logs: servers automatically record technical information such as IP addresses, browser types and access times to keep the service secure and stable.
  4. Analytics: we use Umami to collect anonymous visit statistics, such as page views and device types, to improve the product rather than identify you.

We do not collect information unrelated to these functions or send commercial advertising.

2. How we use information

  1. Sign-in verification: phone numbers receive sign-in codes; email and password are used to register and sign in. Email also receives password reset links.
  2. Contribution attribution: the ledger records who contributed what to which artifact and when. It supports credits, commemorative records and notifications.
  3. Notifications: we may notify you through the site or your contact details about important milestones, such as completion of an artifact you helped build. We do not send marketing messages.
  4. Security: logs help investigate failures and prevent attacks and abuse.

3. Storage

  1. Your personal information is stored on our servers in mainland China and Hong Kong. Uploaded photos are stored in Alibaba Cloud Object Storage Service.
  2. We retain information for as long as necessary for the purposes described here. The contribution ledger is retained long term for attribution and acknowledgments. You may request removal of records linking contributions to your identity.

Except as described above or required by law, we do not provide or sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Your rights

  1. Access and correction: you can view and update your account information in account settings.
  2. Deletion and account closure: you may request removal of identity links in contribution records or closure of your account. After closure, we delete or anonymize your personal information except where the law requires otherwise.
  3. Withdrawal of consent: you may withdraw authorization by stopping use of the relevant function. Withdrawal does not affect earlier processing based on your consent.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in the site footer. We will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Children

Our cultural content is for the general public. If you are under 14, use the service with a guardian’s supervision and consent. If we learn that we collected a child’s information without guardian consent, we will delete it promptly.

7. Security measures

We use HTTPS, access controls, limits on verification attempts and data minimization to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. If a personal-information security incident occurs, we will notify you and relevant authorities as required by law.

8. Policy updates

We may revise this policy and publish changes here with an updated effective date. Material changes will receive a prominent notice.

9. Contact

For questions, feedback or complaints about this policy, contact us using the details in the site footer.
