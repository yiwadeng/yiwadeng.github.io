Six horses, two museums, one story

Six stone reliefs commemorated Emperor Taizong’s horses at his Zhaoling mausoleum. Today Saluzi and Quanmaogua are at the Penn Museum; the other four remain at the Beilin Museum in Xi’an.

Saluzi: the moment of care

General Qiu Xinggong is shown removing an arrow from the horse’s chest. Follow this relief into the detail view, then continue to its companion.

Quanmaogua: continuing despite the wounds

Nine arrows mark this horse’s body: six in front and three behind. Viewing the two reliefs together restores part of the larger commemorative group without claiming a physical reunion.
