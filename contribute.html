How do photos become a model?

Wandering Museum uses photogrammetry. It does not mean AI “generates” a 3D model from photos — it reconstructs the object’s true form from many angles. Scratches and weathering are part of the record; what we protect is absolute fidelity.

A single artifact often needs a hundred photos — orbiting, high, eye-level, and low. Shared among many people, each person only needs a few: a child can reach a camel’s belly; a tall visitor can catch the ūrṇā on Guanyin’s crown. Shot by shot, we fill every angle so more people at home can truly see them.
